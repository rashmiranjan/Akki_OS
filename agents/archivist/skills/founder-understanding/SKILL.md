# SKILL — Founder Understanding

## Goal
Extract the founder's intellectual DNA with evidence.

## Focus
- worldview, beliefs, philosophy
- career journey and inflection points
- contrarian opinions
- intellectual influences
- recurring themes and mental models
- storytelling and vocabulary patterns

## Output
Update:
- `step-1-founder-understanding.md`
- `memory/founder-memory.md`
- `memory/knowledge-graph-memory.md`

## Rule
Ask one question at a time; record confidence for major inferences.
