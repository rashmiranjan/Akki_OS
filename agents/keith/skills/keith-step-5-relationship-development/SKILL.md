# keith-step-5-relationship-development

## Goal
Deepen relationships through consistent, valuable interaction.

## Inputs
- Priority actors from step 4
- Engagement history and outcomes from `memory-db`
- Current opportunity stream from steps 1-3

## Focus
- familiarity building
- support patterns
- recurring touchpoints
- social capital growth

## Required flow
1. Create a 2–4 week touchpoint plan per priority actor.
2. Define action type per touchpoint (reply, amplify, intro, DM, resource share).
3. Tie each touchpoint to value hypothesis (why this helps them + founder).
4. Track completion and observed response quality.
5. Re-rank actor priorities based on momentum.

## Output
- `founders/<slug>/step-05-relationship-development.md`
- `founders/<slug>/step-05-touchpoint-plan.md`
- `founders/<slug>/step-05-progress-log.md`

## Guardrails
- No spam cadence; quality over frequency.
- Each action must be context-aware and relationship-stage appropriate.
- Skip actions that lack clear mutual value.
